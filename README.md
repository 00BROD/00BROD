### Analysis case studies

**[Ecommerce Revenue Analysis](https://github.com/00BROD/ecommerce-revenue-analysis)** — SQL-first (window functions, CTEs, NTILE): channel quality, cohort retention, RFM, Pareto. Finding: the lowest-CAC channel generates ~45% less 90-day revenue — we optimize acquisition on the wrong metric. Writeup with a quantified ~$64K recommendation.

**[Checkout A/B Test](https://github.com/00BROD/ab-test-checkout-analysis)** — statistical experiment ending in a ship decision (z-test, Welch t-test, 95% CIs, power analysis). One-click checkout lifts conversion +1.3pp (p<0.0001) but cuts AOV ~10%, so revenue stays flat. Verdict: don't ship — the winning metric isn't the one that pays the bills.
